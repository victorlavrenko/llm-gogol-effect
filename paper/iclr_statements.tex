\section*{AI Use Disclosure}
Generative AI tools assisted with manuscript drafting and editing, literature discovery, research ideation and methodology discussion, experimental workflow organization, code development and debugging, and discussion of statistical analysis and interpretation. AI tools were also part of the experimental subject matter: the reported synthetic text and model-produced scores were generated by the evaluated language models. Generative AI was not used to prove mathematical claims. The human researcher made the final methodological decisions, executed and inspected the experiments, verified the reported numerical results and citations, determined the interpretations and conclusions, and takes responsibility for the final content and artifacts.

\section*{Ethics Statement}
The quantitative experiments use synthetic prompts, model-generated text, and model-produced scores. No private or personally identifying human data contribute to the reported estimates. The phrase ``average human reader'' is part of the model-scoring instruction and should not be interpreted as a measurement of actual human opinion. The study compares properties of model-generated control signals and does not rank demographic or protected groups.
